# Knowledge datasets

Knowledge is intentionally second.  This directory is the canonical index
and admission boundary, not a permission to train on the current retrieval
corpus.  The authoritative knowledge artifacts remain under
`foundation/artifacts/auto/knowledge` until a separate, disjoint, hash-pinned
knowledge contract is approved.

V60 is identity-only.  A future knowledge campaign must prove that identity
behavior is retained, keep its holdout separate, and pass the independent CPU
retrieval and containment probes before it can become a training input.
